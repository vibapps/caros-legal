CarOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026 · Türkçe

The short version: your records live on your phone. An account is optional. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics SDK in the app.

Who is responsible

CarOS is published by Volkan İlkay Bedel. For anything on this page, including a request to see or delete your data, write to v.ilkay66@gmail.com.

What CarOS stores, and where

On your device, always

Everything you enter: the car itself, odometer readings, fuel and charging, services, expenses, documents and their files, tyres, battery, warranties, incidents and their photos, symptoms, parking, trips, valuations and finance records. Your VIN, number plate, documents and financial records can be placed behind Face ID, and are excluded from widgets, notifications and exports you have not asked for.

If you never sign in, this data never leaves your phone. The app is fully usable that way.

On our server, only if you sign in

Signing in exists for two reasons: a backup if you lose the phone, and the same car on a second device. Your records are then copied to a database we operate on Supabase, under row-level security rules that allow only your own account to read them. Files go to private storage buckets, served only through short-lived signed links.

We store the email address you signed in with. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your address, we receive only Apple's relay address. No password is ever created or stored — sign-in is a one-time code or Apple's own flow.

What CarOS does not do

  • No tracking. No advertising identifier, no ad network, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, nothing sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No analytics SDK.
  • No background location. Location is requested only while you are using the app, only to save where you parked or to attach a place to an incident.
  • No payment details. Purchases are handled by Apple.
  • No reading of your photo library. Only the images you pick.

Your rights

  • Take your data: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Export produces a complete JSON or CSV file on the device. No account, no purchase.
  • Delete it: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Delete all data.
  • Delete the account: Account ▸ Delete my account removes the account, the server copy and the stored files. It cannot be undone.
  • Revoke a share link at any time; revocation is immediate.
  • Under the GDPR and the Turkish KVKK you may also ask us directly for access, correction, erasure or portability, and complain to your local supervisory authority.

How long anything is kept

On your device, until you delete it. On the server, until you delete the record or the account. Ordinary infrastructure backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Children

CarOS is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Apple — App Store, purchases, Sign in with Apple, notifications
  • Supabase — database, authentication and storage for signed-in users
  • RevenueCat — subscription status validation
  • US Department of Transportation (vPIC) — VIN lookups you start yourself

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the app will say so before the change takes effect, rather than only updating this page.
